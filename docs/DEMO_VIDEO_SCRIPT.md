# ComplyFlow AI — demo video script (3:30 target, 4:00 hard cap)

**Recording setup:** 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, no bookmarks bar. Open https://complyflow-copilot.lovable.app and click **Reset demo** before you start.

## 0:00 — 0:25 · Problem hook

“A compliance team opens a regulatory notice, a card-scheme bulletin or a contract amendment, and then spends days turning it into dates, obligations, owners and tasks. Generic AI summarisers make that worse, because one invented deadline destroys the audit trail. ComplyFlow AI turns one document into a working plan — and refuses to show unsupported evidence. It is not legal advice; it is triage for human review.”

## 0:25 — 0:50 · Workspace

Show `/dashboard`: four seeded documents, obligation counts, upcoming dated items, open and overdue tasks.

## 0:50 — 1:20 · Summary and deadlines

Open the highest-risk seeded analysis and show summary, key points and dated items.

## 1:20 — 1:50 · Evidence-backed obligations

Click an obligation and show its verbatim evidence highlighted inside the source document. Explain that unsupported evidence is deleted before storage.

## 1:50 — 2:10 · Risk rationale

Show the 0–100 score, weighted factors and written rationale.

## 2:10 — 2:40 · Owners, plan, checklist

Show suggested owners, implementation plan and change one checklist task to in progress.

## 2:40 — 3:00 · Response and audit trail

Show the new audit-timeline entry and draft acknowledgement.

## 3:00 — 3:20 · Architecture and fallback

On `/new`, run a built-in sample with **Run offline engine**, then show `/how-it-works`. Explain the hosted AI path and deterministic fallback use the same validated schema and grounding stage.

## 3:20 — 3:35 · Close

Return to dashboard: “For a compliance analyst, this is the first pass — what does this document require, by when, and who owns it — in seconds, with every claim traceable to a line in the source.”

## Honesty notes

- Demo documents are illustrative; no affiliation with any regulator, card scheme or company is implied.
- The workspace is browser-local; there is no account system or shared backend.
- The deterministic engine is rule-based triage, not a model.
